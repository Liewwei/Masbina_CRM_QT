# Project Specification: Masbina Smart CRM & Engineering Quotation System

## 1. App Overview & Brand Identity
Build a modern, professional, bilingual (English & Simplified Chinese) B2B Engineering CRM and Automated Quotation Web Application for **Masbina Technology S/B**.

- **Company Name:** Masbina Technology S/B
- **Tagline:** Integrated Security & Automation Solutions
- **Currency:** MYR (RM)
- **Design Aesthetic:** Clean, industrial-tech aesthetic (Slate Navy `#0F172A`, Corporate Blue `#2563EB`, Crisp White `#FFFFFF`, subtle borders, high readability for sales engineers).

---

## 2. Core Business Domain Context (Knowledge Base)
The AI engine inside the app must strictly categorize all client needs and quotation line items using Masbina's official business framework:

### A. Business Scope (主要业务范围)
1. **CCTV Surveillance System** (闭路电视监控系统)
2. **Alarm System** (防盗报警系统)
3. **Access Control System** (门禁及出入管理系统)
4. **Automation System** (自动化系统 - e.g., Auto Gate, Barrier Gate, Smart Controls)
5. **Security Products Retail** (保安系统产品零售)

### B. Engineering Services (工程服务)
1. **Consultation** (客户咨询)
2. **Design** (系统设计)
3. **Supply & Retail** (产品供应及零售)
4. **Installation** (工程安装)
5. **Testing & Commissioning** (测试与系统调试)
6. **Maintenance & Support** (定期维护及技术支援)

### C. Service Workflow Stages (CRM Pipeline Stages)
1. `Consultation` (客户需求分析)
2. `Design & Quotation` (系统设计与方案规划 / 报价中)
3. `Supply & Installation` (产品供应与现场施工)
4. `Testing & Handover` (测试、验收与交付)
5. `Maintenance & Support` (定期维护与售后服务)

## 3. Core Functional Modules

### Module 1: AI Meeting Note Parser (智能拜访记录解析器)
- **Input:** A text area (plus a simulated voice-note transcript paste area or image upload for site sketches/floor plans) where a salesperson enters raw, unstructured post-meeting notes (in English, Chinese, or mixed Manglish/Mandarin).
- **AI Action (Using Gemini API with Structured JSON Output):**
  When the user clicks **"Generate Report & Quotation (生成汇报与报价)"**, Gemini processes the raw notes and simultaneously outputs two structured datasets:
  1. **Sales Visit Follow-Up Report (销售拜访汇报表)**
  2. **Engineering Quotation Draft (工程报价单草稿)**

### Module 2: CRM Sales Visit & Follow-Up Tracker (销售汇报与跟进看板)
- Displays extracted meeting details in a structured form & searchable CRM table:
  - **Client / Company Name** (客户/公司名称)
  - **Contact Person & Phone** (联系人与电话)
  - **Site Type & Location** (场地类型与地点 - e.g., Factory, Office, Residential)
  - **Selected Business Scopes** (涉及业务标签 - multi-select tags from Section 2A)
  - **Current Workflow Stage** (当前工程阶段 - dropdown from Section 2C)
  - **Client Pain Points & Technical Requirements** (客户痛点与技术需求摘要)
  - **Action Items & Next Follow-up Date** (下一步行动与跟进日期)
  - **Deal Status** (`New Lead`, `Quotation Sent`, `Negotiating`, `Closed Won`, `Closed Lost`)
- Users can edit fields, filter by status/stage, and add new records.

### Module 3: Interactive Engineering Quotation Generator (智能工程报价单生成器)
- Automatically populated from the AI extraction, divided clearly into Masbina's engineering structure:
  - **Section A: Equipment Supply & Retail (产品供应明细)** – Item, Brand/Spec, Qty, Unit Price (RM), Total (RM).
  - **Section B: Engineering Services (工程服务费用)** – Automatically includes relevant line items for *System Design*, *Cabling & Installation*, and *Testing & Commissioning*.
  - **Section C: Optional Maintenance Plan (定期维护配套)** – Annual/Quarterly Maintenance & Support option.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - Add/Delete line items, edit quantities, unit prices, and descriptions inline.
  - Real-time calculation of **Subtotal**, **Discount**, **SST (if applicable, toggleable 0% / 8%)**, and **Grand Total (RM)**.
  - **Print / Export View:** A clean, formal "Quotation Preview" modal formatted with **Masbina Technology S/B** header, Quotation Reference No. (e.g., `QT-2026-001`), Validity Date, Terms & Conditions, and a "Print / Save as PDF" button.

## 4. Technical & AI Implementation Requirements
- **Gemini Model:** Use `gemini-2.5-flash` for fast, accurate extraction of unstructured sales notes into strict JSON.
- **Sample Demo Data:** Include a **"Load Sample Meeting Note (加载测试案例)"** button with realistic mixed English/Chinese engineering notes (e.g., *"Met Mr. Tan at Senai Industrial Park factory. Needs 16 units 4MP IP CCTV cameras, 1 NVR, 2 doors biometric access control, and 1 heavy-duty sliding auto gate. Wants full installation and testing by next month. Follow up this Friday."*).
- **UI Layout:**
  - Left Sidebar Navigation: `1. AI Quick Entry (智能录入)`, `2. CRM Follow-Up Board (客户跟进表)`, `3. Quotation Builder (报价单制作)`.
  - Include a Language Toggle (`EN / 中文`) for UI labels while keeping official quotation output professional.
  - Persist data in `localStorage` so the user doesn't lose CRM records or quotations upon page refresh.
